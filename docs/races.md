# Humans

## Identity

Humans are adaptive, ambitious, and fast-moving compared to the other races.

Because of their shorter lifespans, humans often prioritize expansion, innovation, and immediate progress over long-term stability.

Humans rapidly expanded dragon bonding after learning from the Elves.

---

## Lifespan

Humans have the shortest average lifespan among the major races.

Exceptional humans may reach approximately 100 years of age.

This relatively short lifespan heavily influences human culture and politics.

---

## Magic Affinity

Humans possess moderate natural magical affinity.

Some humans are born with magical capability while others are not.

Dragon bonding significantly increased the number of human magic users over time.

---

## Bonding Philosophy

Humans generally believe dragon bonding should be expanded and adapted freely.

Many human cultures view dragon riders as symbols of progress, power, and opportunity.

Human rider factions tend to favor flexibility and rapid growth over rigid tradition.

---

## Bond Formation

Human dragon bonds form quickly and intensely.

Humans rely heavily on emotional openness, adaptability, and instinctive synchronization rather than perfect magical precision.

Because human bonds form rapidly, they are often powerful but emotionally volatile.

Human riders are more likely to:
- develop strong emotional dependency on dragons
- form unusually fast bond growth
- experience instability during emotional stress

Human dragons tend to respond strongly to emotion, ambition, and personal conviction.

---

## Gameplay Identity

Balanced and adaptable.

Default `resonance_style`: `adaptive`

Humans are intended to provide the most flexible and beginner-friendly playstyle.

---

## Gameplay Traits

- faster bond growth
- moderate instability resistance
- adaptable combat options
- balanced magical capability
- flexible progression paths

---

## Dragon Relationship

Human dragons often develop emotionally intense bonds.

Human rider bonds are generally:
- fast-forming
- emotionally driven
- flexible
- occasionally unstable

---

# Elves

## Identity

Elves were the first race to establish stable dragon bonding.

They value balance, discipline, emotional control, and preservation of knowledge.

Elven civilization developed around magical harmony and long-term thinking.

---

## Lifespan

Elves do not naturally die from aging.

However, they may still die from external causes.

Elves reproduce rarely, making births culturally significant events.

---

## Magic Affinity

Elves possess the strongest natural magical affinity among the races.

Almost every elf is born capable of using magic.

Elven magic traditions emphasize resonance, language, emotional control, and precision.

---

## Bonding Philosophy

Elves generally believe dragon bonding is sacred and should remain disciplined and balanced.

Many elves distrust large-scale militarization of dragon riders.

---

## Bond Formation

Elven dragon bonds are formed through deep psychic resonance and true-name synchronization.

Elves approach bonding as a sacred and disciplined process requiring emotional control, patience, and mutual understanding.

Elven bonds typically form more slowly but achieve extremely high synchronization and stability.

Elven dragons often prefer:
- calm emotional states
- disciplined riders
- strong mental synchronization

Among the races, elves achieve the most naturally stable dragon bonds.

---

## Gameplay Identity

Precise, stable, and highly synchronized.

Default `resonance_style`: `harmonic`

Elves favor consistency and control over raw aggression.

---

## Gameplay Traits

- high sync stability
- lower instability gain
- strong magical affinity
- precise dragon coordination
- slower but highly stable progression

---

## Dragon Relationship

Elven dragons often develop calm and deeply synchronized bonds.

Elven rider bonds are generally:
- stable
- disciplined
- mentally synchronized
- spiritually significant

---

# Dwarves

## Identity

Dwarves are structured, resilient, and deeply tradition-oriented.

Their civilization emphasizes engineering, craftsmanship, stability, and long-term planning.

Dwarves were slower to adopt dragon bonding and tend to distrust uncontrolled magical systems.

---

## Lifespan

Dwarves may live for several centuries, with exceptional individuals reaching approximately 700 years of age.

Their long lifespans reinforce their cultural focus on memory and continuity.

---

## Magic Affinity

Dwarves possess lower natural magical affinity than Elves on average.

However, dwarven rune systems allow them to stabilize and structure magical effects with exceptional reliability.

Dragon bonding increased the number of dwarven magic users significantly over time.

---

## Bonding Philosophy

Dwarves generally believe dragon bonding should be regulated and reinforced through structure and engineering.

Many dwarves prioritize control, predictability, and stability over instinct.

---

## Bond Formation

Dwarven dragon bonds rely heavily on structure, ritual, and rune-assisted stabilization.

Rather than relying entirely on instinctive synchronization, dwarves developed systems to reinforce and regulate the bonding process.

Dwarven bonds are highly controlled and resistant to instability, though they may lack the fluid emotional synchronization seen in elven or human bonds.

Dwarven dragons often respond well to:
- consistency
- tactical thinking
- reliability
- disciplined command structures

Dwarven rider traditions emphasize stability over emotional intensity.

---

## Gameplay Identity

Defensive, tactical, and highly controlled.

Default `resonance_style`: `structured`

Dwarves specialize in stability and structured combat systems.

---

## Gameplay Traits

- strong instability resistance
- defensive combat advantages
- rune-assisted abilities
- slower but reliable progression
- high durability

---

## Dragon Relationship

Dwarven dragons often develop disciplined and tactical partnerships.

Dwarven rider bonds are generally:
- controlled
- structured
- highly reliable
- strategically focused

---

# Veyrin

## Identity

Veyrin society is shaped by adaptation, instability, survival, and distrust of centralized authority.

Many Veyrin communities developed under harsh magical conditions and political isolation.

Veyrin culture values personal freedom and resilience over rigid structure.

---

## Lifespan

Veyrin lifespans vary significantly.

Exceptional individuals may reach approximately 200 years of age.

Magical instability has influenced Veyrin biology and society for generations.

---

## Magic Affinity

Veyrin magical capability is highly unpredictable.

Some possess exceptional magical talent while others possess none at all.

Magical instability often affects both Veyrin spellcasting and dragon bonding.

---

## Bonding Philosophy

Many Veyrin believe dragon bonding cannot truly be controlled.

Veyrin riders often treat dragon bonds as dangerous partnerships built through survival rather than authority.

---

## Bond Formation

Veyrin dragon bonds are unpredictable and emotionally extreme.

Many Veyrin bonds form under conditions of stress, danger, survival, or magical instability.

Because of this, Veyrin bonds often fluctuate between extraordinary synchronization and dangerous instability.

Veyrin dragons tend to respond strongly to:
- instinct
- emotional intensity
- survival drive
- uncontrolled resonance

Veyrin riders are more likely than other races to experience:
- bond fractures
- synchronization spikes
- autonomous dragon behavior
- dangerous overbond states

---

## Gameplay Identity

Volatile, reactive, and high risk / high reward.

Default `resonance_style`: `fractured`

Veyrin gameplay focuses on instability management and unpredictable power spikes.

---

## Gameplay Traits

- unstable sync fluctuations
- powerful temporary surges
- high mobility
- unpredictable bond behavior
- aggressive reactive combat

---

## Dragon Relationship

Veyrin dragons often develop emotionally volatile and highly instinctive bonds.

Veyrin rider bonds are generally:
- unstable
- emotionally intense
- difficult to regulate
- capable of extreme synchronization under pressure
